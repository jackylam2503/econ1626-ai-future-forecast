## Project Overview

This project is an interactive web essay forecasting how AI will reshape the Australian supermarket industry by 2027–2030. It argues that AI will drive a productivity revolution across the sector, but without deliberate policy intervention, the competitive advantages of AI risk entrenching Australia's existing supermarket duopoly, displacing routine labour, and concentrating benefits among a small number of large incumbents.

The essay is inspired by the [AI 2027](https://ai-2027.com/) interactive forecast format and blends original economic analysis, data-driven visuals, and policy scenarios into a single public-facing HTML website.

## How to Navigate the Essay

The essay is structured into five sections accessible via the fixed navigation bar at the top of the page:

1. **Mechanisms** — How AI capabilities translate into economic outcomes across the supermarket industry (customer discovery, agentic commerce, supply chain, labour, market structure)
2. **Scenarios** — Three forecast scenarios for 2027–2030 (Upside, Baseline, Downside) with an interactive divergence chart
3. **Policy** — Policy recommendations covering competition, labour transition, and algorithmic pricing governance
4. **Methods** — Data sources, AI tooling disclosure, assumptions, and limitations
5. **References** — Full RMIT Harvard reference list

### Interactive Features
- Typewriter animation on the thesis/landing section
- Hover-reveal scenario summary cards
- Accordion dropdowns for scenario detail
- Interactive bar chart — Global Retail AI Market Growth (2024–2030)
- Interactive line chart — Scenario divergence trajectories (conceptual model)
- Click-to-flip Claude AI disclosure card
- Typewriter assumption tabs (Section 4.3)
- Clickable limitation tabs (Section 4.4)
- Animated store floor plan — ambient visual showing AI transformation of a supermarket (visible on screens wider than 1200px)
